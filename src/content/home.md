I am an Associate Professor in School of Information Engineering at Yangzhou University. I got my Ph.D degree in April 2021 from the College of Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, China, supervised by Prof. Bing Chen. From October 2018 to September 2019, I was a visiting student of Prof. Shui Yu at University of Technology Sydney. My research interest includes AI Security, Federated Learning, and LLM Security. I have published 50+ papers  <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kgDmOAUAAAAJ&hl'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgist.githubusercontent.com%2Fkaimoonstar%2Ffc6849dc5f67fe9974d6f2e6becdee86%2Fraw%2Fcites"></a> at the referred international Security/AI conferences and journals such as CVPR, IJCAI, WWW, TIFS, TDSC, TSC, TR, TAI, COSE. I was chaired over more than 10 projects from the Natural Science Foundation of China, Natural Science Foundation of Jiangsu Province, and China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, etc. I was selected in Jiangsu Provincial Young Sci-Tech Talent Support Program and received Youth Sci-Tech Awards from both the Jiangsu Cyber Security Association and Jiangsu Information Technology Application Society.
